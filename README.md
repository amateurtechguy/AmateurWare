# AmateurWare

A WarioWare styled pixel game where your main goal is to get through levels as fast as you could in each minigame!

Get through as many minigames as you can, it's a free for all! however if you get under 20 you get the loser page👀

## >> Minigame 1:

In this minigame, you have 10 seconds to quickly collect all of the stardust, you can move with the arrow keys and jump with the space key.

## >> Minigame 2:

In this minigame, stardust appears and disappears once you collect it, then another one appears. Collect 4 stardust by left clicking it to win the game.

## >> Minigame 3:

In this minigame, you have to move around and collect 8 stardust before the timer ends.

## >> Minigame 4: 

In this minigame, theres a bar and a needle, the needle moves left and right constantly. Get the needle in the green area before the timer ends to win!

## How does it work?

 You simply just click [here](https://amateurtechguy.itch.io/amateurware), wait for the game to load and then you can play it!

- 6 unique minigames
- Special win and lose screens
- Custom pixel assets and sprites
- Custom animations
